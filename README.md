# 🎬 My Favorite Latest Movies

Here are some of my favorite recent movies that I really enjoyed:

## 1. Deadpool & Wolverine (2024)
- I loved the mix of action and humor, especially seeing **Ryan Reynolds** and **Hugh Jackman** team up again.

## 2. Dune: Part Two (2024)
- The visuals and storytelling were amazing. I liked how it continued **Paul Atreides’** epic journey.

## 3. Inside Out 2 (2024)
- This movie was emotional and relatable. The new emotions like **Anxiety** and **Envy** added depth to the story.

## 4. Bad Boys: Ride or Die (2024)
- It was fun and full of action. **Will Smith** and **Martin Lawrence** still make a great duo.

## 5. Joker: Folie à Deux (2025)
- I found this dark musical interesting and unique, with powerful performances from **Joaquin Phoenix** and **Lady Gaga**.

---

### ❤️ Why I Love These Movies
- Each movie entertained me in different ways — some made me laugh, others made me think, but all left a lasting impression.
